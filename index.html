04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

How do I install Focus Lock?

Download and extract the extension ZIP. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the extracted extension folder.

Which websites can I block?

Focus Lock starts with YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, and TikTok. You can add other website domains to your blocklist.

Does Focus Lock collect my data?

Focus Lock stores its settings and session information locally in your browser. It does not include analytics or send your browsing information to a remote server.

Can I stop a session early?

Yes. You can manually stop a session. Focus Lock is designed to help you build better habits, not to act as a tamper-proof restriction system.

Why can't I install the extension?

Some school or workplace devices restrict extension installation. If Chrome is managed by an administrator, you may need their permission to install extensions.
